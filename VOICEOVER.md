# Sarthi · Final Voiceover Script

~3.5 minutes · warm, confident, plain English · ~520 spoken words
[Stage directions] · *(rubric beat)*

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00–0:10)

> Every year, crores of rupees in India sit unclaimed — in bank accounts, demat
> folios, mutual funds. Not because people forgot. Because their families never
> knew these accounts existed, or who the nominee was.
>
> Sarthi changes that.

*(Resilience — the loss we prevent)*

---

## SCENE 1 · The map (0:10–0:40)

**[show landing → click "Connect via Account Aggregator"]**

> You connect through the RBI-regulated **Account Aggregator** framework. You
> grant consent — you never share a password. In one tap, Sarthi pulls your
> banks, your demat, your mutual funds, your insurance.
>
> In seconds, you're looking at your **Legacy Wealth Map** — every account in one
> place.

*(Bharat — consent-based public digital infrastructure, not screen-scraping)*

---

## SCENE 2 · The number that matters (0:40–1:15)

**[wealth map: gauge 56/100, red "Action needed"]**

> This is the number we built Sarthi around: your **Nominee Health Score**. It
> measures how many of your accounts have a verified nominee.
>
> Those red accounts have **no nominee**. That means if something happens to you,
> they become unclaimed wealth — and your family faces years of legal paperwork.
>
> One tap, and Sarthi takes you straight to CDSL to fix it.

*(Resilience — a measurable score, not a vague warning; usability — one tap, no
forms)*

---

## SCENE 3 · The vault (1:15–1:45)

**[vault: passphrase → encrypt → download → open file → decrypt]**

> Next, lock it all away. The **Family Vault** encrypts your entire footprint —
> **AES-256, right here on your device**. The encryption key never leaves your
> phone or laptop. No server ever sees your data.
>
> You hand the file to your family. When they need it, they open it, type the
> passphrase, and see every account — in seconds.

*(Guardrail + Tech — on-device crypto, privacy by construction)*

---

## SCENE 4 · The affidavit (1:45–2:20)

**[transmission: "Pull from your Wealth Map" → fields auto-fill → generate PDF]**

> Now, the part of inheritance everyone dreads: **transmitting shares** after a
> death. Normally you handwrite a legal affidavit and hope the registrar accepts it.
>
> Sarthi pulls the account from your map, fills in the deceased, the applicant, the
> heirs — and generates the **exact SEBI-format affidavit**, ready to print on
> stamp paper.
>
> And here's the part we're proudest of: **this affidavit is not AI-generated.
> It's a deterministic template of the official format.** Because in a legal
> document, "creative" is a bug, not a feature.

*(Tech Execution + Guardrail — deliberate non-use of AI where it doesn't belong)*

---

## SCENE 5 · The mismatch (2:20–2:50)

**[IEPF: upload KYC + certificate → "Run AI OCR check" → mismatch flagged]**

> For old unclaimed dividends, there's the **IEPF Pre-Validator**. It reads your
> KYC and your decades-old share certificate with AI — and catches the thing that
> rejects most claims: a one-letter name mismatch.
>
> "Ramesh Sharma" versus "Ramesh Sharm." A human glances and misses it. The AI
> doesn't.
>
> Sarthi then drafts the fixing affidavit, and routes you to the correct **Nodal
> Officer** — so your documents go to the right address the first time.

*(Technical Execution — AI used where it earns its place; Resilience — fewer
rejected claims)*

---

## SCENE 6 · Bharat close (2:50–3:20)

**[switch language selector to Tamil — hero + nav flip]**

> And Sarthi speaks your language. **Twenty-three Indian languages.**
>
> Because protecting your family's wealth shouldn't require perfect English.
>
> We built it contract-first — one backend, three clients, no passwords ever in
> the browser — so it can scale from a hackathon to a tool for every Indian
> investor.

*(Bharat — regional language; Feasibility — architecture that scales)*

---

## CLOSING LINE (3:20–3:30)

> Sarthi. Protecting India's wealth — one family at a time.
